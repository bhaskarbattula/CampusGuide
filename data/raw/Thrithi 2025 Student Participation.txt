Thrithi 2025: Student Participation Guidelines and Instructions
All IFHE students are required to strictly follow these guidelines.
Students must upload the parent consent form to the SIS portal by March 18th, 2025, to gain
campus entry on March 22nd, 2025.
For security purposes, ID verification and frisking will be conducted at the main gate and within
the campus. Students are required to carry their IFHE ID cards for the entirety of the
three-day event.
The following items are strictly prohibited inside the campus: bags, water bottles, and all
liquids.
A Zero-Tolerance Policy is in effect regarding the possession, consumption, or distribution of
prohibited substances, including but not limited to alcohol, drugs, and illicit materials, on college
premises during Thrithi. Any violation will lead to immediate expulsion from the festival and
strict disciplinary measures by the college. To ensure the safety and well-being of all
participants, breathalyzer and drug checks will be conducted on students at any point during
the Thrithi 2025 event, and all late entries will be subject to mandatory breathalyzer and
drug checks. Refusal to comply with these checks will result in immediate disciplinary action,
including potential expulsion from the event.
Additional Rules and Instructions:
Wristband Entry Protocol:
● Wristbands will be provided to all students for authorized campus entry.
● Students are required to wear the issued wristband visibly at all times during the event.
● Hostel residents must collect their wristbands within the designated time slot (8:30 AM to
11:00 AM) at either the main gate or the bus student checking area.
● Removal, tampering or absence of the wristband will be interpreted as an attempt to enter
the event as an unauthorized outsider, resulting in immediate expulsion from the event.
● Wristbands can be collected daily from 8:30 AM to 11:00 AM.
● Entry will not be granted to any individual without a valid wristband.
● No day scholar can stay on campus after the last day of the event.
● They cannot bring their parents/ Friends/ relatives on the last day to the campus.
● Bags can be allowed to girl students.
● Students should not raise objections to frisking at the gate.
● Every Student should kindly cooperate with security for Breath Analyser Test .
Gate Timings:
● March 20th & 21st, 2025: The campus gates will close at 11:00 PM. Students exiting
the campus after this time will not be permitted re-entry.
● March 22nd, 2025: The campus gates will close immediately upon your entry. Re-entry
will not be permitted.
● The gates will reopen 15 minutes prior to the event closing. This reopening will occur
immediately before the buses begin their departure from campus.
Identification Cards (ID Cards):
● Students are required to carry their physical IFHE ID cards at all times.
● Digital SIS IDs are not valid for campus entry.
● Entry to the campus will be denied to anyone without a physical IFHE ID card.
Behavioral Expectations:
● Zero Tolerance for Abuse: Any instance of physical or verbal abuse will result in
immediate removal from the college premises.
● Respectful Conduct: Students are expected to maintain decorum and demonstrate
respect towards all individuals throughout the duration of the festival.
● Prohibited Conduct: Provocation, public displays of affection (PDA), and any
involvement in ragging activities are strictly prohibited. Violations of these standards will
result in immediate disciplinary action.
● Permitted Electronic Devices: Only essential electronic gadgets, such as phone chargers
and power banks, are permitted on college premises during the festival. Unauthorized use
of electronic devices not explicitly permitted, including but not limited to cameras, will
result in confiscation and further disciplinary measures.
● To ensure the safety and well-being of all participants, breathalyzer and drug checks may
be conducted on students at any point during the Thrithi 2025 event. Refusal to comply
with these checks will result in immediate disciplinary action, including potential
expulsion from the event.
Restricted Items:
● No snacks, drinks, or water bottles from outside sources are permitted on campus.
● Sharp objects of any kind are strictly prohibited.
● Bags of any kind are prohibited from being brought onto the IFHE campus.
Campus Departure Timings:
● March 20th & 21st, 2025: Day scholars are permitted to remain on campus until 6:00
PM and must depart the campus grounds by 6:00 PM.
● March 22nd, 2025: Day scholars are permitted to remain on campus until 8:00 PM and
must depart the campus grounds by 8:00 PM.
Note: Failure to comply with any of the above rules will be met with strict disciplinary action as
outlined in college regulations.
